By creating an account or placing an order you accept these terms. If you do not accept them, do not use PrintIt.

2. What PrintIt is

PrintIt connects you with independent print shops. You upload a document and choose how you want it printed; we send the job to a shop, the shop prints it, and it is delivered to the address you gave.

The printing is done by an independent business, not by us. We are responsible for the service — taking your order, choosing a shop, pricing it, and getting it to you — and we are the people you deal with if something goes wrong. You do not have to chase anyone else.

3. Your account

  • You must be at least 13 years old to have an account.
  • Give accurate details. A wrong phone number or a wrong address is the most common reason an order does not arrive.
  • Your account is yours. Do not let someone else use it, and tell us promptly if you think somebody has.
  • One person, one account. Accounts created to get round limits or pricing may be closed.

4. Placing an order

  1. You upload your document and choose the paper, colour, sides, copies and any binding.
  2. Our server opens the file and counts the pages. The page count and the price are calculated by us, not by your phone, so the figure you are shown is the figure that will be charged.
  3. You confirm, and the order is offered to print shops that can do that work and are near enough to deliver.
  4. A shop accepts it, prints it, and it is delivered.

We choose the shop, and we do not tell you which shop it was. That is deliberate and it is not something support can look up for you. Before a shop accepts your job it is shown the work and the payment only — not your files, not your file names, and nothing that identifies you.

An order is not guaranteed until a shop accepts it. If no shop takes it — because of the hour, the workload, or the type of job — we will tell you, and you will not be charged.

5. What you may print

You are responsible for what you upload. By sending us a document you confirm that you have the right to print it and to have someone else print it for you.

Do not upload anything that:

  • you do not have the rights to reproduce;
  • is illegal to produce or possess;
  • is counterfeit — money, identity documents, certificates, tickets or official seals;
  • is designed to harass, threaten or defame a person.

We do not read your documents, and we have no system that inspects their contents. But a shop physically prints your file and will see it. If a shop reports an order as illegal, or if we are told about one, we may refuse it and close the account behind it.

6. Prices and payment

  • All prices are in Egyptian pounds and include delivery unless the app says otherwise.
  • The price is worked out from your actual document — its page count and your print settings — plus a flat delivery fee, and is shown to you before you confirm.
  • You can pay by card, or in cash when the order is delivered.
  • Card payments are handled by our payment processor on their own page. We never see your card number.
  • If you chose cash and do not pay on delivery, that balance stays on your account and will stop you placing further orders and closing the account.

7. Cancelling an order

The line is simple, and it is the same line the app enforces:

  • Before a shop accepts your order, you can cancel it yourself from the app, for any reason.
  • Once a shop has accepted, you cannot. By then the shop has opened your files and may already have committed paper and machine time to the job.

If you need to stop an order after a shop has accepted it, email us. We will do what we can, but we cannot promise it.

8. Refunds

If an order is not delivered, arrives badly printed, or is not what you ordered, tell us and we will put it right — normally by reprinting it, or by refunding you.

Refunds are arranged by hand, not automatically in the app. Email hamza2008ismail@gmail.com with your order and what went wrong, and we will reply within 30 days. If we owe you a refund, we have to settle it before your account can be closed, because we cannot send money to an account that no longer exists.

9. Ratings

After an order is delivered you can rate it from one to five stars and leave a comment. Keep comments about the job. We may remove a comment that is abusive or that identifies a person, and we may remove ratings that are not about a real order.

Your uploaded files are deleted automatically 30 days after the order is finished. The privacy policy describes this in full.
