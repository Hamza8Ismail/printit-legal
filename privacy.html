Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

PrintIt takes documents you need printed, sends them to a print shop, and has the result delivered to you. This page says exactly what we hold, who can see it, and how long it stays.

PrintIt is operated by PrintIt, [REGISTERED ADDRESS].

The short version.

  • We collect what is needed to print your document and get it to your door. Nothing else.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDK in the app.
  • Your uploaded documents are deleted automatically once your order has been finished for 30 days. This is a scheduled job, not a promise to get round to it.
  • A print shop cannot see your files, your file names or anything about you until it has accepted your order.
  • You can delete your account yourself, from inside the app or by emailing us. Here is how, and what happens to the data.

1. What we collect

All of it comes from you, or is produced by your own use of the app. We do not buy data about you and we do not collect anything from you before you sign up.

Your sign-in
Depending on how you choose to sign in: an email address and password, an Egyptian mobile number that we send a one-time code to, or a Google or Apple sign-in. If you use Google or Apple, we hold the account identifier they give us and the email address on it — we never see your password for those.
Your profile
Your name and your phone number, if you enter them, and the date your account was created. Both the name and the phone number are optional as far as the database is concerned; you may be asked for them at checkout so a delivery can actually reach you.
Your saved addresses
For each address you save: the label you gave it ("Home", "Campus"), the street, the flat and floor, the area and city, any delivery note or landmark you wrote, the name and phone number of someone else receiving it if you filled those in, and the exact map coordinates of the point you picked. Coordinates are required — we cannot quote or deliver to an address without a real point on the map.
The documents you upload
The PDF files you send to be printed, up to 50 MB each. They are stored in a private bucket — there is no public link to them, and every single read is checked against who is asking. We also store the file's location in that bucket and the number of pages, which our server counts by opening the file. We do not read your documents for any other purpose.
Your orders
What you asked for (paper size, colour or black and white, single or double sided, number of copies, binding and layout choices), the page and sheet count, the price, the payment method and whether it has been paid, the delivery address and coordinates as they were at the moment you ordered, and the time of every status change — submitted, accepted, printing, out for delivery, delivered, cancelled.
Payment records
For cash on delivery, the amount and whether it was collected. For card payments, the amount, the status, the transaction reference from our payment processor, and the confirmation message the processor sends back to us, which we keep so that a disputed charge can be investigated. We never see or store your card number. The card details are entered on the payment processor's own page, not in PrintIt.
Ratings you leave
A star rating from 1 to 5 for a completed order, and your written comment if you left one.
Notifications
The in-app messages we send you about your orders, and whether you have read them.
Requests from outside our delivery area
If you ask us to serve somewhere we do not cover yet, we record the point you dropped on the map and what you asked for, so we know where to expand.
Photos of your packaged order
The print shop photographs your wrapped order before it goes out, as proof of what was handed to the courier.
A counter to stop abuse
How many orders your account has created in a short window, so that one account cannot flood the system. It is a number, not a history.

2. What we do not collect

This is worth stating plainly, because most apps cannot say it:

  • No analytics. There is no analytics, tracking or advertising software in the PrintIt app. We do not know which screens you looked at.
  • No crash reporting service. No third party receives a report when the app misbehaves.
  • No advertising identifiers, no ad networks, and we do not sell or rent anything about you to anybody, for any purpose.
  • No cookies on these pages. This page, and the other two on this site, set nothing, load no fonts from anywhere else, and run no scripts.
  • No contacts, photos, or files other than the ones you pick. When you choose a document, your phone hands us that one file.

Two things that do leave your phone, which you should know about

  • Your location, if you let the app use it. The app can ask your phone for your current position, purely to centre the map on you when you are choosing a delivery point. You can refuse and pan the map yourself. A position only reaches us if you save it as an address or attach it to an order.
  • Map images. The map is drawn with tiles from openstreetmap.org. Loading them means your device contacts that service directly, which, like any website you visit, sees your IP address. We do not send them anything about you or your order.

3. Who else sees any of this

The print shop

This is the part we have designed most carefully, and the rules are enforced by the database itself rather than by the shop's app — so they hold even if somebody tries to go around the screen.

Stage What the shop can see
Before it accepts your order The work only: how many pages and sheets, the print settings, what it will be paid, and roughly how far the delivery point is. It cannot open your files. It cannot see the file names. It does not get your name, your address, or anything that identifies you.
After it accepts Your documents, so it can print them, and the delivery address, so it can be delivered. Your profile name, phone number and email address are not handed over through the app.
If it declines, or is too slow The offer disappears and it never had access to anything else.

We choose which shop gets the work, and we do not tell you which shop handled your order. Your star rating is recorded against the job, not against a shop you named.

Everyone else

  • Our payment processor, for card payments only. You are handed to them to enter your card, and they tell us whether it worked. They receive the amount and the order reference.
  • Our hosting provider. The database, the file storage and the servers that run PrintIt are operated by our cloud hosting provider on our behalf. They hold the data described above because that is where it physically lives. They do not use it.
  • Us. A small number of PrintIt staff can look at an account or an order when they are dealing with a support problem or an unpaid balance.
  • Nobody else. We would disclose something if we were legally required to, and not otherwise.

4. How long we keep things

Your documents are deleted on a schedule

Once an order has been delivered, cancelled, or has failed to find a shop, a clock starts. 30 days later, the files you uploaded for that order are deleted from our storage by an automatic job that runs once a day. The order keeps its receipt — the prices were worked out and saved at the time, so nothing needs to re-read your document ever again — and we record the date the files were destroyed.

The 30 days is a setting rather than something buried in the code, so it can be shortened without a new release. If it changes, this page changes with it.

The one reason a file is kept at all during those 30 days is so that a job can be reprinted if something went wrong with it.

Everything else

  • Your account, profile and addresses: until you delete your account.
  • Your orders: kept as business and accounting records. If you delete your account, they stay but you are removed from them — see the deletion page for exactly what that leaves behind.
  • Notifications and rating comments: until you delete your account, at which point they go.

5. Security

  • Your documents live in a private store with no public link. Every attempt to read one is checked against who is asking and what stage the order is at.
  • Every table is protected by row-level rules in the database, so one customer's account cannot reach another customer's rows even if the app were tampered with.
  • Uploads are limited to PDF files of 50 MB or less.
  • Staff accounts with access to the admin tools require a second factor to sign in.

No system is perfect. If you believe something about your account is wrong or exposed, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we will look at it.

6. Your choices and your rights

  • See what we hold about you. Ask, and we will send it to you.
  • Correct it. Your name, phone and addresses are editable in the app. Anything else, ask us.
  • Delete your account and your data. You can do this yourself. The deletion page explains the steps, what is removed, and what is kept.
  • Withdraw location access at any time in your phone's settings. The app keeps working; you pan the map yourself.
  • Complain. Write to us first — we would rather fix it.

To do any of these, email [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We aim to reply within 30 days. We will check that a request genuinely comes from the account holder before we act on it — otherwise anyone who knew your number could ask for your data.

7. Children

PrintIt is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. If you believe a child has an account with us, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we will remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or who we share it with, we will update this page and change the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will tell you in the app rather than hoping you re-read this page.
